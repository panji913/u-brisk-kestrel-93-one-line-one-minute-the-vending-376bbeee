## How it works

## Why this line

Written against the judge brief, in its order:

1. **Machine speaking** — the line is the machine talking to a passer-by ("I hum all day for strangers"), not a stranger talking about a machine.
2. **The machine's own world** — its hum, its coils, a button (B7), and exact change, instead of love in general.
3. **The laugh lands last** — the punch ("my heart's already spent") arrives in the final words, after the setup.
4. **Short and speakable** — one breath, 153 characters, nothing tacked on.
5. **Charming, not creepy** — cheeky and warm; the machine offers something sweet and admits it is already smitten.
